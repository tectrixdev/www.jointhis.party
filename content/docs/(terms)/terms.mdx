## Section 1: Usage of the subdomain tool

You, as the user, may:

- Use the subdomain tool
- Create records (**under the current limit**)
- Use the records for servers and self-hosted projects

You, as the user, may NOT:

- Use the subdomain tool to host malware
- Bypass any set limits
- Create records to intentionally obstruct others.
  For example, by creating an SRV record where an A record
  was already made to intercept traffic.
- Create man-in-the-middle attacks.
- Impersonate jointhis.party, tectrix.dev, or really
  Anyone else.
- Host illegal content.

By creating a record, you grant us permission to open and look through
your websites, games, ... to prevent abuse. If you want to opt-out, you'll
have to create a ticket **before** creating your record.

The subdomains get moderated frequently by examining the hosted content,
more on this is in the [Privacy Policy](./policy). If you break any of these
rules, you will and can be banned from using the service again. You may
also get banned from the Discord server.

## Section 2: Usage of authentication

The authentication system makes use of nextAuth.

As a user, you may:

- Use the system to log in
- Use the system to log out
- Create records under your account

As a user, you may NOT:

- Use multiple accounts to bypass limits
- Impersonate other people
- Spoofing your id or account to access other users their accounts
- Bypass the authentication system in any way

## Section 3: Vulnerabilities

If you encounter any critical security bugs, please create a confidential
issue on the [GitLab](https://gitlab.com/tectrixdev/www.jointhis.party/-/issues),
or make a ticket on the Discord (support) server. Abusing these vulnerabilities
instead will get you banned, in some cases it may even be illegal and there can
be serious consequences. Merge requests to solve the issue are welcome after
notifying us in a confidential matter.

## Section 4: Discord server

As a member of our discord server, you must follow the rules stated in the
`# ╭╴rules` channel.

You may not, however, break the rules. If you do, you will get moderated and
potentially banned, kicked, muted or warned. Our moderators have the permission
to execute these actions. If they did it wrong, make sure to open a ticket to
appeal and so we can take action against this moderator.

In support tickets, you may ask for any technical question
(related to self-hosting), our support team is trained to help you
and guide you until your problem is resolved. If not, you may always
contact me directly by pinging `@tectrix.dev` in a ticket. If you
need any help with the guides, you may also reach out and make a ticket.

If these support members fail to help you or are rude in any way, please
report it in a staff report ticket. This will put you in direct contact
with higher-rank staff members that will and can take action.
